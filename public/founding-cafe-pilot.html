What AI Café Network provides

  • Premium AI-friendly positioning and launch concept
  • One practical public AI event or workshop
  • QR onboarding and guided guest use cases
  • Event copy, digital promotional materials, and host playbook
  • Simple measurement dashboard and final pilot report
  • Permission-based photography and a public case study

What the café provides

  • A named owner or manager as the pilot contact
  • Reliable guest Wi-Fi and a suitable event area
  • Permission for temporary QR and pilot materials
  • Normal café service during the event
  • Basic baseline and pilot data where available
  • Honest feedback and one decision meeting after 30 days

The first 30 days

WEEK 1Design

WEEK 2Launch

QR experience, promotion, staff briefing, and first public event.

WEEK 3Activate

Follow-up content, guest feedback, and a second community touchpoint.

WEEK 4Measure

Results review, owner feedback, case study, and continuation decision.
