# AI Café Network

**Turning everyday cafés into trusted, AI-friendly places to work, learn, build, and connect.**

AI Café Network is an independent pilot platform for transforming cafés, coworking spaces, hotels, and community venues into accessible local hubs for responsible AI adoption.

The project starts with a focused pilot in **Alanya and Antalya, Türkiye**.

## The problem

AI tools are available online, but many people still lack the environment, confidence, guidance, and local community needed to use them effectively.

At the same time, cafés need stronger reasons for customers to visit, stay, return, and participate in meaningful activities.

## The solution

AI Café Network creates a physical bridge to the AI economy through participating venues that offer:

- QR-based AI onboarding;
- practical workshops and community events;
- guided use cases for work, education, entrepreneurship, and content creation;
- staff enablement and AI adoption support;
- measurable engagement and business outcomes for venue owners.

## Pilot scope

The initial pilot is designed as a **90-day validation program** with **3–5 participating cafés** in Alanya and Antalya.

The pilot will test:

1. whether AI-friendly positioning attracts new visitors;
2. whether workshops and guided onboarding increase repeat visits;
3. whether cafés gain measurable commercial value;
4. whether the operating model can be repeated across multiple venues;
5. whether AI companies gain a reliable physical adoption and education channel.

## Success metrics

The pilot will track:

- participating venues;
- workshop attendance;
- QR onboarding and activation events;
- repeat visits and customer retention;
- qualified business leads;
- changes in foot traffic, session duration, or average spend where data is available;
- venue owner satisfaction;
- willingness to pay for an ongoing program;
- unit economics and operating cost per location.

## Value proposition

### For cafés

- increased visibility and differentiated positioning;
- community-driven customer acquisition;
- longer and more valuable customer sessions;
- workshops, events, and new revenue opportunities;
- practical AI adoption for staff and operations.

### For visitors

- a welcoming place to learn and use AI;
- practical guidance instead of abstract theory;
- local events, peer learning, and professional connections;
- support for work, study, business, and creative projects.

### For AI partners

- a measurable physical distribution channel;
- local education and activation;
- qualified small-business leads;
- real-world adoption data and case studies;
- expansion into tourism-focused and internationally connected markets.

## Initial business model hypotheses

The pilot will validate several possible revenue streams:

- venue subscriptions or monthly program fees;
- paid workshops and corporate training;
- sponsored events and ecosystem partnerships;
- implementation and staff onboarding services;
- membership, day-pass, or team packages;
- qualified lead generation and referral agreements;
- future software tools for venue analytics and program management.

No single revenue model is assumed to be proven before the pilot.

## Product direction

A lightweight platform may later include:

- a directory of verified AI-friendly spaces;
- venue and event discovery;
- QR onboarding flows;
- workshop and community management;
- a venue dashboard;
- engagement and conversion analytics;
- loyalty and membership features;
- a marketplace of AI tools and services for local businesses.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Concept and validation

- define the operating model;
- recruit the first venue;
- prepare onboarding materials;
- launch the first public event;
- establish baseline metrics.

### Phase 2 — Local pilot

- activate 3–5 cafés;
- run the 90-day program;
- collect quantitative results and owner feedback;
- publish a pilot report and case study.

### Phase 3 — Repeatability

- standardize the launch playbook;
- validate pricing and unit economics;
- build the minimum viable platform;
- expand to additional cities in Türkiye.

### Phase 4 — International scale

- partner with hospitality groups, universities, coworking networks, and AI companies;
- expand into tourism-focused markets in Europe and the Middle East;
- develop a repeatable partner and operator model.
