# External partnership status

## Current position

AI Café Network is an independent concept-stage project.

The project team has expressed interest in future partnership opportunities with OpenAI. A general response indicated that a number of partnership programs are still being formalized and that application information may be shared when those programs become publicly available.

This communication does **not** constitute:

- acceptance into a partner program;
- endorsement or sponsorship;
- approval to use OpenAI branding or logos;
- an official commercial, technical, or distribution partnership;
- a commitment that AI Café Network will be accepted into a future program.

## Approved public wording

The following wording may be used in project materials:

> AI Café Network is an independent project. We have expressed interest in future partnership opportunities with OpenAI and other AI providers, but no official partnership or endorsement currently exists.

## Wording that must not be used

Until a written agreement explicitly grants the relevant rights, project materials must not state or imply that AI Café Network is:

- an OpenAI partner;
- powered, approved, certified, sponsored, or endorsed by OpenAI;
- part of an official OpenAI café, education, community, or distribution program;
- authorized to use OpenAI logos or official brand assets.

## Pilot operating model

Participation in the founding café pilot does not require the café to purchase an OpenAI business plan or another enterprise AI subscription.

The initial pilot focuses on:

- organising practical AI events for existing and new visitors;
- activating student, remote-worker, creator, and entrepreneur communities;
- providing QR-based learning and guided use cases;
- measuring attendance, orders, repeat visits, engagement, and feedback.

Visitors may use their own devices and accounts. Separate business subscriptions may be evaluated later only when a real operational use case exists, such as staff training, marketing workflows, customer support, or internal collaboration.

## Review rule

This status must be updated only when there is new written evidence. Any future partnership claim must be tied to the exact agreement, scope, permitted wording, branding rights, effective date, and responsible parties.
